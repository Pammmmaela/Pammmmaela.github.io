I'm inspired by...

Toby Fox and my Dad

They are my entertainment and engineering inspiration. Both fields require programming, because Toby Fox is known for developing games using GameMaker. When I first discovered UNDERTALE, I was truly immersed and fully interested how he made such a cool and story-rich game. That's when my interest in programming started to bear into fruition. On the other side, my dad introduced me to a lot of engineering fields and inspired me to take Electronics and Communications Engineering because I was interested how he can fix electronic devices. I also think it's a useful skill in the age of rising prices, you can be the one to just debug the problem of your phone, for instance.

MILLI, Flow G, Lee Young Ji, Gloc-9

They are my singing inspiration. Their common trait is that all of them are rappers. At first, when I listen to Filipino rap songs, I always think that they are all MONOTONE. But then I was wrong, because when I first saw Lee Young Ji and MILLI in my Instagram reels, they are absolutely COOKING! The swag, costume, timbre, everything! I want to be like them girls. Then afterwards, I was introduced to Flow G's songs. They were not that monotone at all! Especially the fast-paced but catchable "Rapstar" song. It was easy to learn as a quick rap song because it has a catchy melody, no obvious chorus, and memorable instrumental. Though, his similirity with Gloc-9 is that they are both good storytellers. But Gloc-9's more deep and matalinhaga but monotonous in melody.

Coach of my Robotics Club

They are a crucial part of my life even if they don't know it. One time, they were our coach for an innovative robotics contest and it had to include a research paper and a fully developed product. When we won fourth place, I was amazed and happy. Years later after that event, it sparked something inside me. I wanted to develop my own projects too! I want to be the one to write it all up in the paper. Progress, impacts, target market. And not just that! I want to also be the one to come up with the project idea and I want it to have an actual and useful purpose to society. It's like making your own toys but with a prize.
